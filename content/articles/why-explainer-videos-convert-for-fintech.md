Fintech products solve real problems, but they rarely look simple from the outside. Compliance layers, APIs, pricing logic, security claims — the average visitor needs several minutes of reading just to understand what you do. Most won't spend them.

## Complexity kills momentum

Every extra second a visitor spends decoding your product is a second they're not moving toward a decision. Text-heavy pages force the buyer to do the abstraction work themselves. Video does it for them: it sequences the problem, the mechanism and the outcome in 90 seconds, in the order a human brain actually processes a pitch.

## What a good explainer does differently

- **Leads with the pain**, not the feature list
- **Shows the product** instead of describing it — screens, flows, results
- **Compresses trust**: voice, motion and pacing signal credibility faster than copy
- **Ends with one action**, not five

## The compounding effect

The same asset works across the whole funnel: paid ads get higher watch-through, landing pages get longer sessions, sales teams send it instead of a deck. One production, three leverage points.

If your product takes more than two sentences to explain, a 7-day production cycle is usually the cheapest conversion improvement available.
